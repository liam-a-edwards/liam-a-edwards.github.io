Professional Summary

DPhil student in the AIMS CDT at the University of Oxford, working with Ingmar Posner (A2I) and Andrea Vedaldi (VGG) on vision and world modeling for robotic manipulation. Background in mechanical engineering and robot learning, spanning diffusion-policy manipulation, sim2sim validation for humanoid locomotion, and LLM methods for biomedical reasoning.

Experience

  • 2023 - 2026

    Los Angeles, CA

    Robust Machine Learning and Mechanobioengineering
    UCLA
    Advisors: Neil Lin and Cho-Jui Hsieh
    • Developed PairLLM, a comparative fine-tuning approach that teaches language models signed property differences between molecule pairs and recombines them into a common ranking via least squares. Outperformed pointwise prediction on scaffold-disjoint toxicity, permeability, and solubility benchmarks with only 50–100 fine-tuning compounds per property. (First-author manuscript, submitted to PNAS Nexus)
    • Developed an autonomous prompt-evolution framework enabling LLMs to perform interpretable, evidence-linked diagnostic reasoning for cancer pathology classification, and contributed to mechanism-informed language modeling and oxygenated 3D screening that identified berberine–enzalutamide synergy in prostate cancer models. (Published in Communications Medicine and Advanced Science)
  • 2024 - 2025
